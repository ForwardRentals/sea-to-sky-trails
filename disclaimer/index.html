You are responsible for your own safety and for anyone in your care, for planning, for carrying proper equipment, and for following the rules of the land manager and the law. Read the checklist below, and confirm current conditions with the land manager before every trip.

4. Safety checklist

  • Tell someone your plan. Leave your route, group size and expected return time with someone you trust, and check in when you are back. BC AdventureSmart (adventuresmart.ca) has a free trip-plan form.
  • Check conditions the same day. Look at the weather, road and trail conditions, closures and (in snow season) the avalanche forecast on the day you go, not last week. Use the land manager's page for this guide.
  • Set a turnaround time. Pick a time to turn back whatever happens, start early, and carry a headlamp. Most trips go wrong in the last hours of the day.
  • Carry the essentials. Water, food, navigation (map and offline GPS), light, first aid, extra layers, sun protection, a whistle and fire or shelter. A phone alone is not a plan.
  • Emergencies. Call 911. Cell coverage is unreliable or absent in much of the Sea to Sky backcountry, so consider a satellite messenger and know your location.
  • Water and wildlife. Wear a life jacket on the water: cold water can disable you in minutes. Carry bear spray where bears are present, keep food secured and give wildlife space.
Emergency: call 911. Cell coverage is unreliable or missing in much of the Sea to Sky backcountry.
