Overview

Garibaldi Lake is the big turquoise lake behind Rubble Creek that gives Garibaldi Provincial Park its name. By BC Parks' figures the trail to Garibaldi Lake Campground is about 9 km one way with about 900 m of climbing, most of it in the first 6 km.

The same trail system leads on to Black Tusk and Panorama Ridge, so many people treat the lake as the first stop of a bigger day or a night in the campground.

Getting there

Daisy Lake Road

From Highway 99 a 2 km paved road leads to the Rubble Creek lot. In winter BC Parks recommends a 4x4 with high clearance, winter tires and chains, and says the road is plowed but a lower priority than highways.

Day-use pass

Free vehicle day-use passes are required in summer at Rubble Creek. Book at reserve.bcparks.ca/dayuse. You don't need one with a valid overnight camping reservation.

Parking

Parking is not permitted near the Daisy Lake hydro dam access or anywhere blocking snow-plow access.

Rules & safety

Prohibited

BC Parks prohibits motorized vehicles, domestic animals, fires and drones year-round.

Camping

All camping requires a permit and a reservation, year-round, in designated sites only.

Weather

BC Parks warns the weather can be severe and unforgiving, and day visitors should be capable of camping overnight in an emergency.

Below the Barrier

BC Parks says the area below the Barrier is a civil defence zone: do not stop or linger there.

Pack it out

No garbage service. Use the outhouses provided, and bring your own toilet paper beyond the trailheads.

Stay on trail

Staying on trail protects fragile alpine vegetation.

When to go

BC Parks says passes are required during summer months, and in 2025 the window ran from June 13 to October 13 on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays, Mondays and holidays. Check the current season's dates and the snow on the upper trail.

Questions

How far is Garibaldi Lake?

By BC Parks' figures it's about 9 km one way from the Rubble Creek lot to Garibaldi Lake Campground (6 km to the Taylor Meadows junction, then 3 km), with about 900 m of elevation gain.

Do I need a pass?

BC Parks requires a free vehicle day-use pass at Rubble Creek during summer. A valid overnight camping reservation also works. Check current dates and book at reserve.bcparks.ca/dayuse.

Can I bring my dog?

No. Domestic animals are prohibited in Garibaldi Provincial Park, except accredited assistance dogs.

Can I camp at the lake?

Yes, in the designated campground, with a camping permit and a reservation. Camping or staying overnight at or near the Garibaldi Lake parking lot is prohibited.
