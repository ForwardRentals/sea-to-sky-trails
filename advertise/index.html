§ I  ·  The Network

Named destinations, not generic outdoor content.

We don't sell this with a made-up "engaged audience" number. What's true instead: every guide targets one specific, real place that people already search for by name because they've decided to go there — Joffre Lakes, Panorama Ridge, Garibaldi, Russet Lake, the Chief. That's about as high-intent as a reader gets. They're not browsing. They're planning.

§ II  ·  Who This Is For

Businesses that overlap with a real trip.

If what you do sits somewhere along the route — before the trailhead, during the trip, or on the way home — there's almost certainly a guide it belongs next to.

Lift & mountain access

Gondolas and lift-served alpine access — a natural fit for guides that already tell hikers to ride up before they start walking.

Guiding & instruction

Certified guides for terrain we flag as serious — glacier travel, river crossings, avalanche country.

Gear & rentals

Canoes, boards, camping and mountaineering gear — the equipment question every guide eventually answers.

Shuttles & water taxis

Logistics for the trailheads you can't drive to.

Lodges & campgrounds

The night before or the night after — anywhere along the corridor.

Cafés, bakeries & après

The stop on the way in, or the one that's earned on the way out.

For example: our Russet Lake guide already tells hikers to ride the gondola up before walking the Musical Bumps. A lift company sponsoring that guide isn't a banner bolted on top — it's the actual next step in the trip.

§ III  ·  How Placements Work

Scoped to fit, not sold by the impression.

Placements are matched to the guides that actually overlap with your business — not a generic ad slot dropped onto every page.

Guide Sponsorship

One guide, or a few

A clearly marked mention and logo placement on the specific field guide (or handful of guides) that overlap with your trailhead, service area, or product.

Regional Package

A whole region

Placement across every guide in one region — every Whistler guide, every Squamish guide — for businesses that serve a whole area rather than one trailhead.

Network Presence

The full network

Footer and homepage placement across all 31 field guides, for businesses relevant to almost anyone reading this network.
