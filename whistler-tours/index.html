Whistler local guide packages

Spend a day or a weekend with locals who know the lakes, the walks and the quiet spots. Planned on request, with no prices listed: ask for a quote.

How it works. Email us your dates, group size and what you like. We plan a day or weekend around the weather, road access and your comfort level, share it with you, then confirm availability, permits and insurance before you book. Hikes inside provincial parks are offered only where the required permit is held. We don't include the Sea to Sky Gondola or other permitted attractions. Packages

Pick a starting point

Each package is a template: we adjust it to your group. Mix and match.

Whistler Lake Day

A day built around Whistler's prettiest water.

Paddle or swim where conditions allow, walk to a lake viewpoint, and finish with a picnic. We choose the lakes with you based on the weather, the road conditions and how adventurous you feel.

Might include: River of Golden Dreams, Cheakamus Lake, Callaghan Lake (road permitting)

Best for: Couples, families, first-time visitors

Ask about this package

Sea to Sky Lake Tour

A road trip between Whistler, Squamish and Pemberton lakes.

A full day, or two, chasing the corridor's best lakes: your choice of Whistler only, or the wider Squamish and Pemberton set. We plan the order around road access and weather.

Might include: Alice Lake, Brohm Lake, Lillooet Lake, Duffey Lake, Birkenhead Lake

Best for: Photographers, swimmers, road-trippers

Ask about this package

Easy Walks & Waterfalls

Short, scenic and low effort.

A relaxed day of short walks and viewpoints, with time for coffee and photos. Good for families, mixed fitness groups and rest days.

Might include: Train Wreck, Brandywine Falls, Nairn Falls

Best for: Families, mixed groups, rest days

Ask about this package

Off the Beaten Path

Quiet places locals actually go.

We take you to lakes and viewpoints most visitors skip. Some involve rough roads and unmarked trails, so these days depend on current conditions and your comfort level.

Might include: Lillooet Lake, Levette Lake, Callaghan Lake

Best for: Repeat visitors, adventurous travellers

Ask about this package

Hike With a Local

A trail day with a local who knows the conditions.

Choose a hike that suits your group, from an easy lake walk to a bigger day. We handle the planning: passes, parking, timing and what to bring. Hikes inside provincial parks are offered only where a required permit is held.

Might include: Cheakamus Lake, Garibaldi Lake, Rainbow Lake

Best for: Hikers who want local knowledge

Disc Golf & Easy Day

A round of disc golf with a swim or a walk.

A laid-back day built around disc golf, plus a nearby lake or short walk. Tell us your experience level and we match the pace.

Local Food & Short Walks

Good food, easy walks.

A day that mixes a couple of easy walks with local food stops. We suggest places based on what you like and what is open, and we don't list restaurants here.

A mix of the above, planned around your group: a lake day, an easy walks day and a bigger adventure, with time to rest. We share the plan in advance.

Questions

Before you ask

How much do the packages cost?

We don't list prices: every day is planned around your group, the season and the route. Email us with the dates, group size and what you want, and we'll send a quote.

Do you take people on hikes?

Yes, where it is allowed. Guiding in provincial parks needs a commercial park-use permit, so hikes inside parks are offered only where the required permit is held. Otherwise we plan the day on other trails and lakes or point you to a licensed guide.

Do you go up the Sea to Sky Gondola?

No. Operating attractions like the gondola requires operator permits, so we don't include it in packages. Our guides are there to plan your own visit.

Can you recommend restaurants?

Yes: we suggest local favourites when we plan your day, based on what you like and what is open. We don't list them on this page.

Where do you operate?

Whistler, Squamish and Pemberton, plus the roads between them. Tell us where you're staying.
