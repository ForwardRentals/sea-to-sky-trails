Squamish Hikes, Lakes & Paddles

Granite, glacier-fed rivers and a tidal estuary at the head of Howe Sound: the hikes, swim lakes and paddles within a short drive of downtown Squamish.

17 guides

Guides in this region

This page collects 17 Sea to Sky field guides, including Alice Lake, Echo Falls & Echo Lake, The Squamish Estuary, Watersprite Lake, Elfin Lakes, The Mamquam. 9 of them are easy outings and 5 are hard or serious days, so there is something for most abilities. Each card opens a full guide with routes, seasons, access and rules.
