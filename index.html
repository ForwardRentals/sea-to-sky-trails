Questions

Planning a Sea to Sky trip

What is Sea to Sky Trails?

An independent set of field guides to the lakes, hikes and paddles between Howe Sound and Pemberton, with honest logistics: roads, passes, tides, seasons and who can get you there.

What are the best hikes in the Sea to Sky corridor?

It depends on the day you want. The Stawamus Chief and Black Tusk are big days, Cheakamus Lake and Shannon Falls are easy outings, and Elfin Lakes and Wedgemount Lake are classic hut trips. Browse the collections to compare.

Where can I paddle a canoe near Squamish and Whistler?

Alice Lake, the Squamish Estuary, Brohm Lake, Lucille Lake, Callaghan Lake and the River of Golden Dreams are among the guides with paddling notes, from beginner-friendly water to trips where a canoe is the only way in.

Do I need a day pass for Garibaldi or Joffre Lakes?

BC Parks has used free day-use passes on busy trails such as Rubble Creek in Garibaldi and Joffre Lakes. Rules and dates change, so always check the current BC Parks page before you go.

When is hiking season in the Sea to Sky?

Low-elevation trails and shoreline paddles run most of the year. Alpine trails are usually snow-free from July to September. Each guide lists its own season.

Are the guides free to read?

Yes. The guides are free to read. Some operators support the guides, and sponsored placements are always marked.
