When to Hike the Sea to Sky: A Seasonal Guide

Timing matters as much as the trail. Here's how the season works in the corridor, from the guides we've checked.

By Jeremy Allen · Squamish-based adventure photographer · Updated November 18, 2026

Do your own research first. This article is general information, last checked November 18, 2026. Confirm conditions, passes and rules with the land manager before you go. Terms of use & disclaimer.

Low-elevation trails

Shoreline walks and valley trails such as the Squamish Estuary and the Train Wreck run most of the year, though winter brings rain, ice and short days.

Alpine trails

Alpine routes like Black Tusk and Panorama Ridge are usually snow-free from about July to September. Snow can linger, and weather is severe at times.

Summer passes

BC Parks uses free day-use passes in summer on busy trails: see which hikes need a pass.

Winter and snow safety

Avalanche terrain needs training and gear. Read our safety guide.

Questions

When is hiking season in the Sea to Sky?

Low-elevation trails run most of the year. Alpine trails are usually snow-free from July to September.

Do I need a pass in summer?

Some trails do. Check BC Parks.
