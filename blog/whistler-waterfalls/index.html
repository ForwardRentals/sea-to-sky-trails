Whistler Waterfalls Worth the Walk

You can see several waterfalls near Whistler in a day without a big hike. Here are the main three, with the details BC Parks gives.

Do your own research first. This article is general information, last checked November 18, 2026. Confirm conditions, passes and rules with the land manager before you go. Terms of use & disclaimer.

Brandywine Falls (70 m)

BC Parks says the falls are 70 m, a 10 to 20 minute walk each way. The lower falls area is closed because of unstable slopes. Leashed dogs allowed. Full guide.

Nairn Falls (60 m)

BC Parks gives a 3 km round trip (1.5 km each way) to the 60 m falls, at least an hour. About 32 km north of Whistler. Full guide.

Rainbow Falls

Rainbow Falls is at the trailhead for the Rainbow Lake route. A shorter Rainbow Falls loop is reported at about 2.1 km with about 150 m of gain. Rainbow Lake guide.

Waterfall safety

Never wade or climb near the water. BC Parks warns at Shannon Falls and Brandywine that rocks and edges are extremely slippery.

Questions

What is the tallest waterfall near Whistler?

Brandywine Falls is 70 m according to BC Parks.

Can I swim at the falls?

BC Parks warns against wading or approaching the water at Brandywine and Shannon Falls.
