Best Photography Spots on the Sea to Sky

I shoot adventure sports in this corridor, and these are the places I'd point a visiting photographer to first. All of them are described with official access info in our guides.

By Jeremy Allen · Squamish-based adventure photographer · Updated November 18, 2026

Do your own research first. This article is general information, last checked November 18, 2026. Confirm conditions, passes and rules with the land manager before you go. Terms of use & disclaimer.

Garibaldi Lake and Panorama Ridge

The turquoise colour of Garibaldi Lake is the shot, and Panorama Ridge gives the view from above. Both are long days, so plan your light and daylight carefully.

Brandywine Falls

A short walk with a good platform for a long exposure. Guide. Stay behind the fences.

Shannon Falls and the Chief

Easy access to dramatic granite and water. Shannon Falls, Stawamus Chief.

Tips for the light

  • Early and late light is softer, but plan your hike so you're never descending in the dark without a headlamp.
  • A polarizer helps with water and wet rock.
  • Mountain weather changes fast: pack rain protection for your camera.

Respect the place

Stay on trail, keep clear of edges, and never trade safety for a shot. Read our safety guide. Drones are restricted in parks: check BC Parks rules first.

Questions

Where is the best view in the Sea to Sky?

Panorama Ridge above Garibaldi Lake is among the best, but it is a long day. Easier options include Brandywine and Shannon Falls.

Can I fly a drone?

Drone rules apply in provincial parks and protected areas, so check with BC Parks before you fly.
