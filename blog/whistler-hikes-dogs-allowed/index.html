Whistler Hiking With Dogs: The Rules

Dog rules around Whistler catch a lot of visitors out. Some of the area's best-known hikes ban dogs outright. Here is what the land managers say.

Do your own research first. This article is general information, last checked November 18, 2026. Confirm conditions, passes and rules with the land manager before you go. Terms of use & disclaimer.

Where dogs are not allowed

  • Garibaldi Provincial Park: BC Parks prohibits domestic animals, except accredited assistance dogs. That covers Black Tusk, Garibaldi Lake, Panorama Ridge, Cheakamus Lake, Wedgemount Lake and Singing Pass.
  • Rainbow Lake: not allowed, because it is part of Whistler's watershed (guide).
  • Joffre Lakes: BC Parks does not allow dogs or other pets.
  • Cirque Lake basin: not allowed, because it is grizzly habitat.

Where leashed dogs are allowed

  • Brandywine Falls: leashed at all times, not in beach areas or park buildings.
  • Nairn Falls: leashed at all times, not in beach areas or park buildings.
  • Callaghan Lake: leashed, only at the campground, the day-use area or in a boat.

Always check signs

Rules change, and land managers post local signs. Confirm with the park or municipality before you go, and read our terms of use & disclaimer.

Questions

Can I hike with my dog near Whistler?

Yes at some places, such as Brandywine Falls and Nairn Falls on a leash, but not in Garibaldi Provincial Park, Joffre Lakes or at Rainbow Lake.

Are dogs allowed at Garibaldi Lake?

No. BC Parks prohibits domestic animals in Garibaldi Provincial Park, except accredited assistance dogs.
