Big Day Hikes Near Whistler

These hikes are long, steep and committing. The numbers below come from BC Parks' Garibaldi trail table, summed by segment.

Do your own research first. This article is general information, last checked November 18, 2026. Confirm conditions, passes and rules with the land manager before you go. Terms of use & disclaimer.

How to prepare

  • Start early and set a turnaround time.
  • Carry the essentials and a headlamp, and be ready to camp out in an emergency. BC Parks says day visitors should be capable of camping overnight.
  • Check the weather, the passes and the trail conditions the same day.

Which one first?

Garibaldi Lake is the most approachable of the group at about 9 km one way. Wedgemount is short but very steep. Black Tusk and Panorama Ridge are the longest days. See our 3-day itinerary.

Questions

What is the hardest hike near Whistler?

By BC Parks' figures, Panorama Ridge is the longest at about 29 km return with about 1,560 m of climbing, and Wedgemount is the steepest.

Can I do Black Tusk as a day hike?

Many hikers do: about 28 km return, often 8 to 10 hours. Start early and book your day pass.
